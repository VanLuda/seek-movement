### Participant Code of Conduct

**The Seek Movement**

**Last Updated:** January 2026

The Seek Movement exists to create Christ-centered spaces marked by safety, respect, and genuine community. All participants are expected to uphold the following standards.

#### 1. Respectful Behavior

Participants are expected to treat others with kindness, respect, and dignity, regardless of background, beliefs, or personal experiences.

Harassment, intimidation, or discriminatory behavior will not be tolerated.

#### 2. Personal Responsibility

Participants are responsible for:

- Their own physical, emotional, and spiritual well-being
- Respecting personal boundaries
- Participating only in activities aligned with their comfort and ability

#### 3. Safety & Compliance

Participants must follow all retreat guidelines, safety instructions, and directions provided by staff or volunteers.

Unsafe behavior, including disregard for instructions or creating risk to others, is grounds for removal.

#### 4. Substance Use

Illegal substances are strictly prohibited at all times.>
The use or possession of alcohol is **not permitted on the property** during the retreat.

Violation of this policy is grounds for **immediate removal from the retreat**, at the discretion of The Seek Movement, **without refund**.

#### 5. Removal from Event

The Seek Movement reserves the right to remove any participant whose behavior is disruptive, unsafe, or inconsistent with the values of the retreat.

Removal may occur without refund.

#### 6. Agreement

By registering for and attending an event or retreat, participants agree to abide by this Code of Conduct.
